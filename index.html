ClaudeUsage

A tiny macOS menu bar app that shows your Claude weekly and five-hour usage limits at a glance. The dropdown has usage bars, reset times and an "at this pace" forecast, and the little pixel mascot changes colour as you get closer to the limit.

  • Swift
  • macOS 13+
  • Open source
